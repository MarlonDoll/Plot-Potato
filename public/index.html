How to Play

  1. 🥔
    Set the scene

    Each player creates a setting and cast of characters for their story.

  2. ✍️
    Pass it around

    You'll write a section of someone else's story. You only see the one section written just before yours — nothing earlier.

  3. 🙈
    Stay in the dark

    The setting and characters are always shown, but author names stay hidden until the end of each story.

  4. 🎉
    Big reveal

    Once all rounds are done, each story is read aloud prompt by prompt. Chaos guaranteed.
